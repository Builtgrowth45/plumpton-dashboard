User Data Deletion

The Plumpton Revival Campaign Dashboard does not store any user data on any server. All data accessed through Meta (Facebook) and Google APIs is displayed in your browser in real-time and is not saved, cached, or retained after your session ends.

No personal information, access tokens, ad account data, or analytics data is stored in any database, file system, or third-party service.

How to remove access

To revoke the App's access to your accounts:

  1. Facebook / Meta: Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → find "Plumpton Dashboard" → Remove
  2. Google: Go to Google Account → Third-party apps → find "Plumpton Dashboard" → Remove Access

Once removed, the App will no longer be able to request any data from your accounts.
